Calculation notes

Media CAC is not fully loaded CAC

Activation CPA = media spend ÷ unique activations. Media CAC = media spend ÷ unique new paying accounts. Fully loaded CAC appears only when all allocated acquisition costs are supplied.

CPM = spend ÷ impressions × 1,000
CPC = spend ÷ clicks = CPM ÷ (1,000 × CTR)
Display activations = budget ÷ CPC × click→signup × signup→activation
Agentic activations = budget ÷ acceptance price × acceptance→activation

CPM alone cannot determine CAC. Per-format accounts must be deduplicated by account ID in production; this offline model assumes no cross-format overlap because no identity map is supplied. Observed attribution ≠ incremental lift.
